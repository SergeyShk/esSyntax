The model of esSyntax (data/es-syntax.bin) and the check file of its forward pass
(data/check.conllu: the first 200 sentences of the development part with the parses of the model)
are made from the training and development parts of the Universal Dependencies treebank
UD_Spanish-AnCora, release 2.19 (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Spanish-AnCora),
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0). The
treebank comes from the AnCora corpus of the University of Barcelona (http://clic.ub.edu/corpus/):

  Taulé, M., M. A. Martí, M. Recasens (2008). AnCora: Multilevel Annotated Corpora for Catalan
  and Spanish. Proceedings of the 6th International Conference on Language Resources and
  Evaluation (LREC 2008). Marrakesh, Morocco.

and was converted to Universal Dependencies by Héctor Martínez Alonso and Daniel Zeman.

The part-of-speech tags and features the model is trained on are given by esMorph
(https://github.com/SergeyShk/esMorph).

The esSyntax model is an adaptation of that data and is distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
