The model of esSyntax (data/es-syntax.bin) is made from the training and development parts of the
Universal Dependencies treebanks, release 2.19 (https://universaldependencies.org):

- UD_Spanish-AnCora (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Spanish-AnCora), licensed under
  the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC BY 4.0). The treebank comes from
  the AnCora corpus of the University of Barcelona (http://clic.ub.edu/corpus/) and was converted
  to Universal Dependencies by Héctor Martínez Alonso and Daniel Zeman:

    Taulé, M., M. A. Martí, M. Recasens (2008). AnCora: Multilevel Annotated Corpora for Catalan
    and Spanish. Proceedings of the 6th International Conference on Language Resources and
    Evaluation (LREC 2008). Marrakesh, Morocco.

- UD_Spanish-GSD (https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Spanish-GSD), licensed under the
  Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0). The treebank
  was annotated and converted by Google, by Miguel Ballesteros, Héctor Martínez Alonso, Ryan
  McDonald, Elena Pascual, Natalia Silveira, Daniel Zeman, Joakim Nivre and John Bauer (original
  treebank contributors: Yvonne Quirmbach-Brundage, Adam LaMontagne, Milan Souček, Timo Järvinen
  and Alessandra Radici):

    McDonald, R., J. Nivre, Y. Quirmbach-Brundage, Y. Goldberg, D. Das, K. Ganchev, K. Hall,
    S. Petrov, H. Zhang, O. Täckström, C. Bedini, N. Bertomeu Castelló, J. Lee (2013). Universal
    Dependency Annotation for Multilingual Parsing. Proceedings of ACL 2013.

The part-of-speech tags and features the model is trained on are given by esMorph
(https://github.com/SergeyShk/esMorph).

The esSyntax model is an adaptation of that data and is distributed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International License (CC BY-SA 4.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

The check file of the forward pass of the model (data/check.conllu) holds the first 200 sentences
of the development part of UD_Spanish-AnCora with the parses of the model. Its text is that of
UD_Spanish-AnCora only, and the file is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
